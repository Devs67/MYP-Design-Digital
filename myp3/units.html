Unit 1 and Unit 2 — what each unit is, the concepts behind it, and the summative task.

U1

Creative App Design & Logic

Digital Design · Start July, last week · 19 weeks

Key concept
Development*
Related concepts
Evaluation, Adaptation
Global context
Scientific and technical innovation — adaptation, ingenuity and progress

“The development of scientific and technical innovations is driven by the constant evaluation and adaptation of existing ideas.”

Goal

You are a junior UX designer at a student-led tech studio tasked with creating a functional app prototype that solves a real problem for your community (school, neighbourhood, or city).

Role

Junior UX/UI designer at a tech startup.

Audience

A panel of judges including your teacher, peers, and an invited community stakeholder (e.g. school librarian, local NGO representative).

Situation

Your studio is entering a local “Youth Innovation Challenge.” The most original, user-centred, and technically sound prototype wins funding for development.

Product

A multi-screen interactive app prototype (Figma, Marvel, or paper prototype) supported by a written design rationale (400–600 words) explaining your design choices, iterations made, and how feedback shaped your final design. Include a short pitch (2–3 min) on presentation day.

U2

Food Tech Innovators

Food Design · Start Jan 1st week · 19 weeks

Key concept
Communities*
Related concepts
Resources, Collaboration
Global context
Personal and Cultural Expression — artistry, craft, creation, beauty

“Effective collaboration and the sustainable management of resources allow communities to thrive.”

Goal

Design, develop, and present an innovative food product that responds to a real community need — focusing on sustainability, cultural identity, or resource-conscious cooking.

Role

A food innovator and product developer working within a small culinary team.

Audience

A panel of community judges including your teacher, peers, and an invited stakeholder (e.g. school canteen manager, local chef, or community health worker).

Situation

Your team has been commissioned to pitch a new food product to a community food initiative. The product must be practical, culturally meaningful, and made with sustainable or locally sourced ingredients.

Product

A tested and refined food product presented with a design portfolio including: a research report on the community need, annotated recipe development log showing iterations, a sustainability and resource analysis, and a 3-minute pitch to the panel with food tasting.
