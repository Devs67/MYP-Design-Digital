This year’s units

Unit 1 and Unit 2 — what each unit is, the concepts behind it, and the summative task.

U1

Creative App Design & Logic

Digital Design · Start July, last week · 19 weeks

Key conceptDevelopment*
Related conceptsEvaluation, Adaptation
Global contextScientific and technical innovation — adaptation, ingenuity and progress
Statement of inquiry“The development of scientific and technical innovations is driven by the constant evaluation and adaptation of existing ideas.”
GoalYou are a junior UX designer at a student-led tech studio tasked with creating a functional app prototype that solves a real problem for your community (school, neighbourhood, or city).
RoleJunior UX/UI designer at a tech startup.
AudienceA panel of judges including your teacher, peers, and an invited community stakeholder (e.g. school librarian, local NGO representative).
SituationYour studio is entering a local “Youth Innovation Challenge.” The most original, user-centred, and technically sound prototype wins funding for development.
ProductA multi-screen interactive app prototype (Figma, Marvel, or paper prototype) supported by a written design rationale (400–600 words) explaining your design choices, iterations made, and how feedback shaped your final design. Include a short pitch (2–3 min) on presentation day.
Criteria
A · Inquiring & Analysing B · Developing Ideas C · Creating the Solution D · Evaluating
U2

Food Tech Innovators

Food Design · Start Jan 1st week · 19 weeks

Key conceptCommunities*
Related conceptsResources, Collaboration
Global contextPersonal and Cultural Expression — artistry, craft, creation, beauty
Statement of inquiry“Effective collaboration and the sustainable management of resources allow communities to thrive.”
GoalDesign, develop, and present an innovative food product that responds to a real community need — focusing on sustainability, cultural identity, or resource-conscious cooking.
RoleA food innovator and product developer working within a small culinary team.
AudienceA panel of community judges including your teacher, peers, and an invited stakeholder (e.g. school canteen manager, local chef, or community health worker).
SituationYour team has been commissioned to pitch a new food product to a community food initiative. The product must be practical, culturally meaningful, and made with sustainable or locally sourced ingredients.
ProductA tested and refined food product presented with a design portfolio including: a research report on the community need, annotated recipe development log showing iterations, a sustainability and resource analysis, and a 3-minute pitch to the panel with food tasting.
Criteria
A · Inquiring & Analysing B · Developing Ideas C · Creating the Solution D · Evaluating
