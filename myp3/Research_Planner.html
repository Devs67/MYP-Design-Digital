Self-check before submitting

  • Each research area is clearly linked to my specific problem, not generic
  • Each area has at least 2 questions
  • Across all my questions, at least one Factual, one Conceptual, one Debatable appears
  • No question can be answered by the first line of a search result
  • Every question names a specific source and says whether it's primary or secondary
  • Every justification explains how the answer shapes a design decision
