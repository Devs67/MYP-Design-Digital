U1

Coding for Change

Start July 22 · 19 weeks

Key concept
Systems*
Related concepts
Adaptation, Form
Global context
Identities and relationships — lifestyle choices

“Systems are designed through adaptation and form to support human identities, relationships, and well-being by responding to changing physical, psychological, and social needs. Or systems and products can be created in different forms to support healthy lifestyle choices and improve well-being.”

Goal

Design and develop a functional multi-page HTML website that supports human identities, relationships, and well-being by responding to changing physical, psychological, and social needs.

Role

You are a junior web designer commissioned by a youth wellness organisation.

Audience

Teenagers aged 13–16, school community, parents.

Situation

Many young people struggle to access reliable and engaging information about health, transitions, and relationships. Your client needs an accessible website to support them.

Product/Performance

A coded HTML/CSS website (minimum 4 linked pages) with navigation, media, clear layout, and researched content.

Standards for Success

Effective inquiry, justified design ideas, functional code, visual consistency, accessibility, usability testing, and thoughtful evaluation.

U2

Designing for Everyone

Start Nov 22 · 19 weeks

Key concept
Development*
Related concepts
Function, Ergonomics
Global context
Fairness and development — imagining the hopeful future (inclusivity focus)

“Through development, designers use function and ergonomics to create fair and inclusive products that imagine a more hopeful future for diverse users.”

Goal

Design and prototype a product or solution that meets the functional and ergonomic needs of a user who is often overlooked by mainstream design — such as a person with a disability, an elderly user, a left-handed person, or someone from a non-dominant cultural background.

Role

An inclusive design consultant at a product development studio.

Audience

A community review panel including peers, teacher, and a guest representative from a disability, accessibility, or equity-focused organisation.

Situation

Your studio has been commissioned by a “Design for All” initiative to create a product prototype that demonstrates how inclusive, ergonomic thinking can lead to better outcomes for everyone — not just the target user.

Product

A physical or digital prototype of the inclusive product, accompanied by: a research report, annotated design development iterations, an ergonomic and functional analysis, and a 3-minute presentation to the panel explaining how the design imagines a more hopeful future.
