MYP 1 Design · Formative assessment 2

Design elements and principles

21 questions · 35–40 minutes · Most questions mark themselves. A few need a written answer, and Part C is a poster.

How it works

Three parts

Part A checks what you know. Part B gives you five posters with a problem to fix. Part C is a poster you design yourself. Answer every question, then press Check my score. Your teacher marks the written answers and the poster.
