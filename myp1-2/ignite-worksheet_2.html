Identify The Problem

Ignite Goes Pan-India — fill this in as your team investigates. Your answers save automatically on this device as you type.

The Situation

Last year, Ignite — Sreenidhi International's innovation event — ran successfully across Hyderabad. This year, the school wants Ignite to go pan-India: reaching students, teachers, and schools all across the country, not just here. Before your team can design Ignite's new brand and promotion, you need to find out exactly what's stopping that from happening right now.

01

My Problem

What is the problem, in your own words? Try: “Right now, ____________________ don't know about Ignite because ____________________.”

02

Who Is Affected?

Q1

Ignite needs to reach students, teachers, and school administrators. Which group is least likely to have already heard of Ignite — and why?

Q2

Are they at a school like ours, or in a different city or state?

Q3

How would a school outside Hyderabad normally hear about a national student event?

03

When / Where?

Q1

Before Ignite's registration window, or once it's already closing?

Q2

Through what channel — social media, an email to schools, word of mouth, a website?

Q3

Does it reach past Hyderabad and still get missed, or does it not really travel past Hyderabad at all?

04

Prove It's Real

The Rule of Three — collect one of each.

A NUMBER

e.g. how many cities/states were at Ignite last year vs. across India, or how many people you asked hadn't heard of it

A VOICE

e.g. a quote from someone about whether they'd heard of Ignite

A NOTICING

e.g. something you noticed about how Ignite is currently promoted

05

Why Does This Need Solving?

What happens if Ignite stays Hyderabad-only again?

What could happen if Ignite reaches students everywhere in India?

Who benefits most if this problem gets solved?
