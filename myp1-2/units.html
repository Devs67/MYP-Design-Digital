MYP 1

U1

Promoting Innovation through Digital Media

Digital Design · July, last week · 19 weeks

Key concept
Communication*
Related concepts
Form, Collaboration
Global context
Identities & Relationships

“Communicating an idea effectively depends on considering diverse perspectives and using form deliberately to build relationships with individuals across different identities and communities.”

Goal

To design a complete brand and promotional campaign — logo, poster, and a set of promotional materials that communicates Ignite, the school’s innovation event, to students and schools across India, maximizing pan-India registrations.

Role

You are in charge on a student branding and communications team helping launch Ignite as a national event for your school (Sreenidhi International).

Audience

Students (Pr), teachers (Sr), and school administrators across India who may register for or attend Ignite.

Situation

Last year, Ignite ran successfully at a Hyderabad level. This year the school wants to scale it pan-India, reaching more schools and students than ever before, and needs strong visual branding plus a strategic promotional plan to make that happen.

Product/Performance

Students design an event logo, a promotional poster, and a promotion plan. Concepts are tested with a real audience.

Standards

Assessed on all four MYP Design criteria (A–D).

MYP 2

U1

Wokwi — sensor unit

Electronics · “Sensing Our World: Engineering Solutions for Community Wellbeing” · Jan 6th · 19 weeks

Key concept
Communities*
Related concepts
Function, Innovation
Global context
Fairness and Development — imagining the hopeful future

“Innovative systems function most meaningfully when they are designed to serve the needs of the communities they belong to.”

Goal

Design and simulate a sensor-based solution using Wokwi that addresses a real community need (e.g., air quality monitor, flood alert system, accessibility aid).

Role

You are a junior engineer at a social impact tech startup.

Audience

A local community board or NGO evaluating low-cost tech solutions.

Situation

Your community faces a recurring environmental or social challenge. Using Wokwi, prototype a sensor unit that monitors or responds to this issue and present it as a viable, fair solution.

Product/Performance

A working Wokwi simulation + a short pitch (written or presented) explaining how the system functions, who it serves, and why it promotes fairness.

U2

Pixels Through Time: Designing Games That Communicate History

Digital Design — Coding · Code.org, Game development · July 22nd · 19 weeks

Key concept
Communication*
Related concepts
Form, Adaptation
Global context
Orientation in time and space — epochs, eras, turning points and ‘big history’

“Communication is shaped by how ideas are adapted and how form is used to represent and interpret history through interactive experiences.”

Goal

Design and build a playable game using Code.org that is set in or inspired by a significant historical era or turning point in ‘big history’.

Role

You are a game designer commissioned by a digital history museum.

Audience

Students aged 10–14 visiting the museum’s interactive digital gallery.

Situation

The museum wants engaging games that help young visitors understand key turning points in history — not just facts, but the feel of an era. Your game must communicate the context of that time through its design, mechanics, and storytelling.

Product/Performance

A fully playable Code.org game with a short designer’s note explaining: the historical era chosen, how the game’s form and mechanics reflect that period, and what adaptations were made to make it accessible and engaging for the audience.
