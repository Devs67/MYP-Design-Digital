MYP 1

U1

Promoting Innovation through Digital Media

Digital Design · July, last week · 19 weeks

Key conceptCommunication*
Related conceptsForm, Collaboration
Global contextIdentities & Relationships
Statement of inquiry“Communicating an idea effectively depends on considering diverse perspectives and using form deliberately to build relationships with individuals across different identities and communities.”
GoalTo design a complete brand and promotional campaign — logo, poster, and a set of promotional materials that communicates Ignite, the school’s innovation event, to students and schools across India, maximizing pan-India registrations.
RoleYou are in charge on a student branding and communications team helping launch Ignite as a national event for your school (Sreenidhi International).
AudienceStudents (Pr), teachers (Sr), and school administrators across India who may register for or attend Ignite.
SituationLast year, Ignite ran successfully at a Hyderabad level. This year the school wants to scale it pan-India, reaching more schools and students than ever before, and needs strong visual branding plus a strategic promotional plan to make that happen.
Product/PerformanceStudents design an event logo, a promotional poster, and a promotion plan. Concepts are tested with a real audience.
StandardsAssessed on all four MYP Design criteria (A–D).
Criteria
A · Inquiring & Analysing B · Developing Ideas C · Creating the Solution D · Evaluating

MYP 2

U1

Wokwi — sensor unit

Electronics · “Sensing Our World: Engineering Solutions for Community Wellbeing” · Jan 6th · 19 weeks

Key conceptCommunities*
Related conceptsFunction, Innovation
Global contextFairness and Development — imagining the hopeful future
Statement of inquiry“Innovative systems function most meaningfully when they are designed to serve the needs of the communities they belong to.”
GoalDesign and simulate a sensor-based solution using Wokwi that addresses a real community need (e.g., air quality monitor, flood alert system, accessibility aid).
RoleYou are a junior engineer at a social impact tech startup.
AudienceA local community board or NGO evaluating low-cost tech solutions.
SituationYour community faces a recurring environmental or social challenge. Using Wokwi, prototype a sensor unit that monitors or responds to this issue and present it as a viable, fair solution.
Product/PerformanceA working Wokwi simulation + a short pitch (written or presented) explaining how the system functions, who it serves, and why it promotes fairness.
Criteria
A · Inquiring & Analysing B · Developing Ideas C · Creating the Solution D · Evaluating
U2

Pixels Through Time: Designing Games That Communicate History

Digital Design — Coding · Code.org, Game development · July 22nd · 19 weeks

Key conceptCommunication*
Related conceptsForm, Adaptation
Global contextOrientation in time and space — epochs, eras, turning points and ‘big history’
Statement of inquiry“Communication is shaped by how ideas are adapted and how form is used to represent and interpret history through interactive experiences.”
GoalDesign and build a playable game using Code.org that is set in or inspired by a significant historical era or turning point in ‘big history’.
RoleYou are a game designer commissioned by a digital history museum.
AudienceStudents aged 10–14 visiting the museum’s interactive digital gallery.
SituationThe museum wants engaging games that help young visitors understand key turning points in history — not just facts, but the feel of an era. Your game must communicate the context of that time through its design, mechanics, and storytelling.
Product/PerformanceA fully playable Code.org game with a short designer’s note explaining: the historical era chosen, how the game’s form and mechanics reflect that period, and what adaptations were made to make it accessible and engaging for the audience.
Criteria
A · Inquiring & Analysing B · Developing Ideas C · Creating the Solution D · Evaluating
